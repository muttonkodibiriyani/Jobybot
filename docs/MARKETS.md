# Markets & Visa Notes (for Indian professionals)

Based on 2025-2026 immigration data and tech job market analysis.

## 🥇 Primary Market: 🇦🇪 UAE

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Currency** | AED (~3.67 to USD) |
| **Visa** | Employment visa easy for existing UAE residents; new arrivals get 2-year visa once employer signs offer |
| **Language** | English working language; Arabic optional |
| **Senior PM salary** | AED 25-50k/month tax-free (USD 80-160k/yr) |
| **Sponsorship** | Very common — most employers handle it |
| **Tech demand** | 🔥🔥🔥 Massive — Talabat, Careem, Noon, G42, banks all hiring AI/data/PM |
| **Tax** | 0% income tax |
| **Best cities** | Dubai (tech hub), Abu Dhabi (gov/oil/G42) |

## 🥈 Secondary Markets (in order of difficulty for Indians)

### 1. 🇸🇬 Singapore (easiest after UAE)
- **Visa:** Employment Pass (EP) for >SGD 5500/mo. Tech.Pass for top talent (no employer needed).
- **Language:** English official
- **Senior PM:** SGD 100-200k/yr
- **Indian community:** Very large (Tamil is an official language)
- **Tax:** 15-22% (vs India's 30%+)
- **Catch:** EP harder to renew vs PR; recent tightening of S-Pass for non-graduates

### 2. 🇩🇪 Germany
- **Visa:** EU Blue Card — €45,300/yr threshold (€41,041 for shortage occupations like IT). Easy 21 months → PR.
- **Language:** English in tech; German useful but not mandatory at SAP, BMW, Zalando, N26
- **Senior PM:** €80-130k/yr (Berlin/Munich)
- **Tax:** 30-42%
- **Best cities:** Berlin (startup), Munich (enterprise), Hamburg
- **Job seeker visa:** 6-month visa to job-hunt on the ground — useful

### 3. 🇳🇱 Netherlands
- **Visa:** Highly Skilled Migrant (HSM) — €5,008/mo gross (€3,672 for <30yr). Employer must be IND-recognised sponsor.
- **Language:** English fluent everywhere; Dutch only needed for civil service
- **Senior PM:** €70-110k/yr
- **30% ruling:** First 5 years tax-free on 30% of salary (huge!)
- **Best cities:** Amsterdam (tech), Eindhoven (deep tech), Rotterdam
- **Catch:** Housing is very tight in Amsterdam

### 4. 🇮🇪 Ireland
- **Visa:** Critical Skills Employment Permit — €32k threshold (€64k for streamlined). PR after 5 years.
- **Language:** English official
- **Senior PM:** €70-130k/yr (Dublin)
- **Tax:** ~40% (high but offset by EU access)
- **Why amazing:** Dublin = European HQ for Google, Meta, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, LinkedIn, Salesforce, Stripe
- **Catch:** Dublin housing extremely tight

### 5. 🇨🇦 Canada
- **Visa:** Express Entry (CRS points), Canadian Experience Class. Open Work Permit for spouse.
- **Language:** English (Quebec needs French)
- **Senior PM:** CAD 110-160k/yr
- **Tax:** ~40%
- **Best cities:** Toronto (banking+tech), Vancouver (tech), Montreal
- **Why amazing:** Most Indian-friendly PR pathway (1-3 years to PR via CEC)
- **Catch:** Cold winters; competitive PM market in 2025-2026

### 6. 🇦🇺 Australia
- **Visa:** Subclass 482 (TSS, employer-sponsored), Skilled Independent 189 (points), ENS 186 (PR)
- **Language:** English (must pass IELTS for points)
- **Senior PM:** AUD 140-200k/yr
- **Tax:** ~32-45%
- **Best cities:** Sydney (fintech), Melbourne (corp HQs), Brisbane (growing)
- **Catch:** Geographically isolated; high cost of living

### 7. 🇬🇧 UK
- **Visa:** Skilled Worker visa (employer-sponsored, £38,700 threshold from 2024)
- **Language:** English
- **Senior PM:** £80-130k/yr (London)
- **Tax:** ~40-45%
- **Best cities:** London (fintech, consulting)
- **Why:** Massive financial+tech sector
- **Catch:** Salary thresholds tightening; competitive market post-Brexit

## Strategy

If you have UAE residency: stay there, but apply to Singapore + Germany + Netherlands in parallel as backup.

If you don't have UAE residency: hit **Singapore + Germany + Canada** hardest — they have the largest Indian tech communities and clearest visa pathways.

Skip USA/USA H1B-dependent markets unless you already have a green card / EAD. The 2025-2026 H1B environment is too volatile for cold outreach.
