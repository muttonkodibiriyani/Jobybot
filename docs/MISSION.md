# Why JobyBots exists

> *"You shouldn't have to spend three hours every night refreshing LinkedIn just to get noticed. An AI should be doing that for you while you have dinner with your family."*

## The night this started

It was a Sunday in late 2024 in Dubai. I'd spent **eight years** as a working
product manager in MENA retail — leading data products across 100+ brands:
loyalty platforms, AWS data lakes, Power BI dashboards, Copilot Studio AI
agents — work that paid the bills and won awards.

Then a wave of layoffs hit the industry. Not just one team — friends across
several companies I'd worked with for years. Most of them spoke three
languages, held visas tied to their employer, and had families depending on
the next paycheck.

I watched them open LinkedIn at 6 AM, scroll until midnight, copy-paste the
same cover letter into the 87th Easy Apply form of the week, and still get
**zero replies**. Not because they weren't qualified — they were among the
sharpest people I've ever worked with — but because the modern job market
is a numbers game stacked against humans.

- An average open role on LinkedIn gets **250+ applications** in the first hour.
- The first 25 applicants typically get all the interviews.
- Recruiters spend an average of **7.4 seconds** scanning a résumé.
- 75% of qualified candidates are never even seen because the ATS filters them out.

The math is impossible. Unless you can apply to **200 well-tailored jobs a
day** — which no human can sustain — you're playing a losing game.

## The bet I made

I asked a simple question: *what if the AI did the grind, and the human
did the interview?*

That meant building something that could:

1. **Read your résumé once** and understand what you actually do.
2. **Scan 8 job boards every 30 minutes** (LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, Bayt,
   GulfTalent, RemoteOK, Glassdoor, plus 40+ company ATS feeds).
3. **Score every job 0–100** against you using a real LLM (Gemini Flash),
   not keyword matching.
4. **Write a custom cover letter** for matches above 70% — quoting the JD,
   referencing your résumé, sounding like *you* not a robot.
5. **Validate every recruiter email** before sending so you don't get
   flagged as spam.
6. **Stay safe on GDPR markets** (EU, UK, Sweden) — apply via the official
   site only, never cold-email.
7. **Run 100% on your laptop.** Your résumé, Gmail password, and Gemini
   key never leave your machine. No SaaS, no cloud, no monthly fee.

That's JobyBots.
