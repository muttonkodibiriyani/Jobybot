# JobyBots — Refund Policy

**Effective date:** May 2026. **Window:** 7 days from delivery.

## TL;DR
Submit a refund request at <https://jobybots.com/refund> within 7 days of
receiving the installer and we refund 100% to the same UPI / card within
5 business days. No phone calls required.

## Who is eligible?
* Any individual customer who paid full price (₹2,999 INR / $49 USD /
  AED 179) on jobybots.com.
* Within 7 days of the order being marked **approved** (we count from when
  you received the installer email, not the payment date).

## Who is not eligible?
* Customers who have requested two or more refunds previously.
* Bulk / team / reseller licenses (these have a custom contract).
* Customers who attempted to resell or redistribute the installer.

## How to request

1. Go to <https://jobybots.com/refund>.
2. Provide:
   * Order ID (format `JB-XXXXX-XXXX`, in your purchase email).
   * Your registered email.
   * Your phone number.
   * Reason (at least 10 characters — helps us improve).
3. Tick the consent box and submit.

You'll receive an automated acknowledgement within 30 minutes.

## How long the refund takes
* Acknowledgement: **30 minutes** (automated).
* Manual review: **1–2 business days**.
* Refund processed to UPI / bank: **1–5 business days** depending on your
  bank.

## Where the money goes
Refund returns to the **same UPI account or card** that made the payment.
For UPI we reverse to the VPA from the original transaction. For Stripe
card payments we issue a refund through Stripe directly — the bank
typically posts it within 5 business days.

## After the refund
* Your order record is deleted within 24 hours.
* Your license is revoked. Continuing to use the installer after a refund
  violates the license terms.
* You can re-purchase any time.

## Exceptions (case-by-case after 7 days)
We review fair-use refund requests **outside the 7-day window** for:
* The installer is broken on your specific OS version.
* A bug we cannot resolve in 14 days makes the bot unusable.
* You purchased a duplicate license by mistake.

Email `tharakesh.iitp@gmail.com` with order ID and details.

## Chargebacks
Please don't issue a chargeback before contacting us. We respond same-day
to email and we honour the 7-day window without questions. Chargebacks
trigger Stripe disputes that take 60–120 days to resolve and cost
$15-$25 in dispute fees that don't help anyone.
