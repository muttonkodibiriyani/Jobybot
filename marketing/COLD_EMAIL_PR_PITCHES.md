## Cold-email hygiene

- ALWAYS personalise the first 2 sentences. No exceptions.
- Subject line: specific, no clickbait, ≤80 chars.
- Body length: ≤180 words. Anything longer is unread.
- Sent from a Gmail with a 6-month-old sender history. Not from a fresh account.
- Track opens + clicks with UTM in the demo URL.
- Reply rate target: 12-18%. Below 8% means the pitch needs rewriting.
